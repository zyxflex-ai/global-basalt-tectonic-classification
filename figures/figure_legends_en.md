# Journal of Earth Science main-figure legends

**Figure 1 | Publication-aware data curation and evaluation workflow.** Publication identities were normalized and checked across databases before samples were assigned to publication-level groups. A stratified group split separated development data from a frozen holdout without sharing a `CV_GROUP`. Stable-50 and measurement-coverage criteria were applied identically to both branches. Model selection used grouped cross-validation only within development data; the holdout was used once for final evaluation, publication-level bootstrap analysis, and frozen-model SHAP interpretation.

**Figure 2 | Sample-random splitting inflates apparent model performance.** **a,** Accuracy, balanced accuracy, and macro-F1 under publication-grouped and sample-random repeated fivefold cross-validation. Points denote ten partitions per scheme; larger symbols and bars show medians and interquartile ranges. **b,** Sample-random minus grouped differences from 2,000 publication-cluster bootstrap resamples; points show medians and bars show 95% percentile intervals. **c,** Class-specific F1 inflation across ten partitions. **d,** Fraction of validation samples whose publication also occurs in training; points denote 50 folds per scheme and black lines show medians.

**Figure 3 | Class-specific performance of the frozen XGBoost model on the publication-group holdout.** The confusion matrix is normalized within each true class. Each cell reports row percentage and sample count (n = 10,086). Rows are true tectonic settings and columns are predicted settings.

**Figure 4 | Class-specific geochemical-feature importance.** Panels **a–h** show the eight highest-ranking features for CAB, IAB, IOAB, BABB, MORB, OIB, OPB, and CFB. Bars report mean absolute SHAP values across all 10,086 holdout samples for the corresponding raw class-margin output. Horizontal scales are independent among panels and should be used for within-class rankings only.

**Figure 5 | Publication-level transfer performance on the frozen holdout.** **a,** XGBoost accuracy for each publication-class combination. Boxes show medians and interquartile ranges; whiskers extend to 1.5 times the interquartile range; points are individual publication-class units. **b,** Publication-level accuracy against log10 sample count for all 462 holdout publications. The horizontal line shows the publication-equal mean (0.7937); Spearman ρ = -0.089. This exploratory analysis did not inform model development.

**Figure 6 | Time-closed temporal and source-coverage tests.** **a,** Performance after feature selection, completeness filtering, tuning, and fitting were confined to publications dated 2010 or earlier, with 2011–2025 publications held out (11,871 samples from 519 publications). Points are estimates and bars are 95% publication-cluster bootstrap intervals. **b,** Sample-weighted and publication-equal recall for eight tectonic classes; bars show 95% publication-bootstrap intervals for publication-equal recall. **c,** Publication-equal MORB recall for the mixed-source model and a GEOROC-only training ablation on database-exclusive frozen-test subsets. Bars show 95% publication-bootstrap intervals; n denotes independent publications. Panel **c** measures dependence on represented source-composition space, not a causal database effect.
